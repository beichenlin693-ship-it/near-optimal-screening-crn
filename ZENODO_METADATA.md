## Description

This archive contains reproducibility materials for the manuscript
'Near-Optimal Screening under Fixed Simulation Budgets: Preservation,
Efficiency, and the Role of Common Random Numbers.'

The study evaluates fixed-sample and adaptive simulation-screening procedures
under hard computational budgets, distinguishing whole-set near-optimal
preservation, screening efficiency, and final recommendation performance.
It also examines how common random numbers and explicitly paired inference
affect screening behavior.

The archive includes source code, experimental configurations, deterministic
seeds, derived simulation inputs, machine-readable summaries, and scripts
for reproducing the reported tables and figures.

The WNBA component is an empirically informed simulation benchmark based on
public-source data. Price elasticity is externally calibrated, and
application performance is evaluated against a finite simulation reference.
The repository does not claim transaction-level demand calibration,
causal elasticity estimation, operational validation, or a real-market
optimal ticket price.
